## 1. Which organization operates as "Jekka AI", and is it the AI company meant?

### Takeaway
"Jekka AI" almost certainly means **Jekka.ai**. It is a China-rooted, globally marketed startup that sells enterprise **text and voice AI agents** (customer service, sales and shopping assistants, heavily e-commerce oriented). It operates in mainland China through **杭州西格莫伊德智能科技有限公司 ("Sigmoid")** in Hangzhou and lists offices in Singapore, Hong Kong and Wilmington, DE. A Shopify app published by "Hello Jekka" is probably an early product from the same team. Every other "Jekka" result is an unrelated namesake.

## 4. Stated mission and dated history; accelerators and pivots

### Takeaway
**Mission:** reliable, human-level ("真人级") enterprise AI agents, summed up in the tagline "Powers Every Touch between Human and AI". The stated philosophy is that **reliability comes before raw intelligence**: an agent must be stable, correctable, have memory and keep learning.

**History (dated):**
- 2023-12: GPT-4-based Shopify FAQ and chat app;

**No dated 2026 announcements were found.** There is no evidence of YC or Techstars. "Google for Startups" is listed as a backer, but the program type is unknown.
