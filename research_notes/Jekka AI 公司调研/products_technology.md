## 2. What products does Jekka offer, what problems do they solve, and for whom?

### Takeaway
As of Oct 2026, jekka.ai shows four product lines:
- **Jekka Quanta Engine | Heisenberg**, the core engine, marketed as "zero hallucinations".
- **Jekka Engine | Voice AI Agent**, for inbound and outbound calls.
- **DouDou**, "human-level" AI customer service for e-commerce.
- **DuoDuo (哆哆)**, a "human-level" AI shopping assistant for e-commerce.

Earlier products were a multilingual chatbot (2024) and a GPT-4 Shopify FAQ/chat app (from Dec 2023, now unavailable on the Shopify App Store). The company is B2B only. Its core market is e-commerce, especially Chinese cross-border sellers on Shopee and similar platforms targeting Southeast Asia and Latin America. It also claims work in logistics, the "unmanned economy" (无人经济), and custom copilots for US enterprises.

## 3. What technology underpins the products: own models, fine-tuned open models, or third-party APIs? What is publicly visible about the architecture and stack?

### Takeaway
The public record shows a progression:
- **2023:** an explicitly GPT‑4-based app.
- **2024:** "general LLMs plus self-developed models".
- **2025–26:** marketing of a proprietary "**Jekka‑Markov** large model" and "**Heisenberg**" engine, with a self-developed "neural-network-like control structure" for SOP adherence, debuggability and "zero hallucination".

The company does not disclose its base models. I found no papers, repositories, model cards, benchmarks, APIs or SDKs, so none of the "own model" or "zero hallucination" claims can be independently checked.

### Cited Findings
- 2023–24: the Shopify app was branded "GPT4" and generated FAQs "with ChatGPT‑4" in one click, with chat that learns from store policies (a retrieval-style setup over merchant content) — [PickYourApp](https://pickyourapp.com/products/hellojekka-ai-sales-chatbot); [Store Leads](https://storeleads.app/reports/shopify/app/hellojekka-ai-sales-chatbot)
- A third-party directory says the 2024 chatbot "uses GPT‑4". This is a single, low-authority claim — [Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai/tool/jekka-ai-chatbot); [Chatbene](https://chatbene.com/jekka-ai-chatbot/)
- Aug–Sep 2024: the CAREC deck describes the product as built on large language models plus self-developed models — [CAREC deck](https://www.carecprogram.org/uploads/6.-CAREC-AI-Seminar-2024-Jekka-Shopify.pdf)
- Current DuoDuo site:
  - "自研 Jekka‑Markov 大模型提供可控、可验证的 AI 对话能力" (the self-developed Jekka-Markov model provides controllable, verifiable AI dialogue)
  - claims "100% 零幻觉": no wrong answers or improvisation in customer-service and transaction scenarios

  Source: [jekkaduoduo.com](https://www.jekkaduoduo.com/)
- Current jekka.ai: "Jekka Quanta Engine | Heisenberg — 32-bit enterprise-grade floating-point computing. Zero hallucinations. Pinpoint precision." The site gives no architecture detail — [jekka.ai](https://jekka.ai/)
- Oct 2025 interview, on architecture:
  - AI on its own cannot solve hallucination, so Jekka built a **"类神经网络的控制结构"** (neural-network-like control structure) at the model level. It combines the LLM's ability to generalize with a **white-box**, human-like reasoning process.
  - "White-box debugging" means that when an answer is wrong, the team can quickly trace the root cause and adjust how the model understands the case.

  Sources: [Sohu transcript write-up](https://m.sohu.com/a/948536090_355029); [53AI](https://www.53ai.com/news/zhinengkefu/2025102892538.html)
- Oct 2025 interview, on design philosophy:
  - Reliability ("靠谱") matters more than peak intelligence.
  - What makes AI reliable is "worst-case stability and **variance control**", not its occasional ceiling.
  - Aaron says 99% of the market bubble comes from over-belief in scaling laws, and he prefers to learn AI's limits bottom-up from test results.

  Sources: [小宇宙](https://www.xiaoyuzhoufm.com/episode/68ff8d9101567203214ec437); [Podwise](https://podwise.ai/podcasts/9813)
- The founder names the main difference from other agent companies as "拥有能提供真人级别 AI 的底层模型的能力" (owning the underlying-model capability to deliver human-level AI) — [Podwise](https://podwise.ai/dashboard/episodes/5698963)
- Scale claims (company, Oct 2025):
  - SOP-following ability and an 80%+ resolution rate come from "每天十万甚至百万轮对话、电话交互" (100k to 1M dialogue and phone turns per day)
  - "每天处理数百万次文本与语音交互，日均消耗数亿 token" (millions of text and voice interactions a day, hundreds of millions of tokens a day)

  Sources: [Podwise](https://podwise.ai/dashboard/episodes/5698963); [53AI](https://www.53ai.com/news/zhinengkefu/2025102892538.html)
- Integrations disclosed: platform official APIs (Shopee); WhatsApp and email (Shopify app); channels per the 2024 deck: e-commerce platforms, web chatbot, email, social media, phone — [Shopee product page](https://service.shopeecb.cn/software-support/2005/product-116); [Shopify-Spy](https://shopify-spy.com/apps/hellojekka-ai-sales-chatbot/); [CAREC deck](https://www.carecprogram.org/uploads/6.-CAREC-AI-Seminar-2024-Jekka-Shopify.pdf)
- No open-source footprint: a GitHub search for "jekka" (2026-10-16) found no Jekka.ai organization, only personal accounts and repos. One repo, `Lishijie0/go-zero-jekka` (Go, created 2025-07-23; go-zero is a Chinese Go microservice framework), could not be inspected (access restricted), and nothing ties it to the company — [GitHub user search](https://github.com/search?q=jekka&type=users); [repo](https://github.com/Lishijie0/go-zero-jekka)
- Searches for "Jekka Heisenberg / Markov" returned only unrelated academic work, such as a paper on absorbing-Markov-chain decoding against hallucination. I found no Jekka paper, patent or Hugging Face presence — [example unrelated arXiv result](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.20340)

### Inferences (my analysis, for an agent-systems reader)
- **Likely architecture** (not disclosed by the company): an orchestration and control layer that constrains LLM generation with explicit dialogue states or SOP graphs.
  - The "Markov" branding, "controllable and verifiable", "SOP-following", "white-box debugging" and "variance control" all fit a state-machine or state-transition controller that wraps the LLM.
  - Around it, the agent would likely use retrieval over the catalog and policies, tool calls to platform APIs (order status, payment reminders, cart recovery), and a check or guardrail step behind the "zero hallucination" claims.
  - The voice product would likely wrap the same engine with speech recognition, text-to-speech and telephony.

  This is the "LLM + deterministic SOP" pattern that the founder's own reference points (Sierra, Decagon; see Q6) also emphasize.
- **Own foundation model? Probably not from scratch.** The 2024 materials say "LLMs + self-developed models". There are no model cards, papers or benchmarks, and team size and funding are unclear. A proprietary control layer or fine-tuned models on top of third-party or open-source base models seems more plausible than a pretrained foundation model. "自研大模型" in Chinese marketing often covers fine-tuned or wrapped systems. Moderate confidence only.
- The "**32-bit floating-point**" claim is technically opaque. At most it suggests full-precision (unquantized) inference. Numeric precision does not by itself prevent hallucination, so this reads as marketing language.
- **Token arithmetic, as a weak signal:** "millions of interactions" against "hundreds of millions of tokens" a day works out to roughly 10²–10³ tokens per interaction. That is low for an agent that sends a long system prompt, SOP and retrieved context on every turn. It suggests compact prompts, prompt caching or smaller in-house models handling much of the traffic, or simply loose rounding.
- Google appears among partner logos (Q5). That could mean Google Cloud or Gemini use or a startup program, but nothing confirms it. I found no evidence of video understanding or multimodal pipelines.

### Gaps
- Which base LLMs are used today (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Qwen, DeepSeek, others), and whether they are fine-tuned — not disclosed.
- Voice stack (speech recognition, text-to-speech, telephony provider) and latency — not found.
- How the "99%+ accuracy" and "zero hallucination" figures were measured — not found.
- No public API, SDK, developer docs, changelog, engineering blog, patents or job ads naming the stack were found. A Chinese job-board search returned nothing for Jekka.
- No BuiltWith or Wappalyzer data was retrievable.

## 6. What do users, reviewers and developers say, and how does Jekka compare with alternatives?

### Takeaway
Independent reception is **almost nonexistent**. The only real user reviews are 7–8 on the legacy Shopify app, averaging about 4.6–4.8. They praise its human-like help and translation, and include one 2-star review. G2 has "not enough reviews". I found no Product Hunt launch, no Reddit or HN threads, and no independent Chinese reviews of DuoDuo. Comparisons come only from the founder, who benchmarks against Sierra and Decagon, and from listings that place DuoDuo beside Chinese cross-border support tools such as Duoke (多客).

### Cited Findings
- The Shopify app rating differs by source:
  - 4.8 from 8 reviews — [Store Leads](https://storeleads.app/reports/shopify/app/hellojekka-ai-sales-chatbot)
  - 4.7 from 8 reviews — [Shopify (cs locale)](https://apps.shopify.com/hellojekka-ai-sales-chatbot?locale=cs)

Against US agent platforms (Sierra, Decagon), it argues it owns its model stack and keeps SOP behavior low-variance. None of these comparisons has been tested independently.
